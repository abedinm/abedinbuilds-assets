Restaurant Website Kit — Edit Guide

Everything lives in one file: index.html. Open it in any text editor. No installs, no build tools, no monthly fees.

1. Make it yours (5 minutes)

  1. Restaurant name: search for Saffron House (and Saffron in the logo) and replace with your name. Update the <title> and <meta name="description"> at the top.
  2. WhatsApp ordering: search for 8801XXXXXXXXX — it appears in 3 buttons. Replace with your WhatsApp number: country code + number, no plus sign, no spaces (e.g. 8801712345678). The pre-filled message after ?text= is editable too.
  3. Address & phone: edit the "Find us" card, and put your real address into the Google Maps link (https://maps.google.com/?q=YOUR+ADDRESS).
  4. Hours: edit the rows in the "Opening hours" table. The highlighted row uses class="today" — move it to whichever row you want emphasized.
  5. Daily special banner: the orange strip under the hero — edit the text or delete the whole <div class="strip"> line.

2. Edit the menu (the part you'll touch most)

Each dish is one small block — copy-paste to add, delete to remove:

<div class="item">
  <div>
    <div class="nm">Dish name</div>
    <div class="ds">Short description</div>
  </div>
  <div class="price">9.90</div>
</div>
Currency: prices show a $ automatically. To change it, edit one line at the top: --currency:'$'; — set it to '৳', '€', 'Rs ', anything.

3. Swap the images

All images are in images/. Replace them with real photos of your food — keep the same file names and you never touch the code. JPG/PNG work the same: src="images/hero.jpg".

  • hero.svg — main food shot (landscape)
  • dish-1.svg … dish-4.svg — gallery (square photos look best)

4. Rebrand colors & fonts (30 seconds)

One marked block at the top of index.html:

:root{
  --brand:#d95d27;     /* buttons, accents */
  --green:#2f5d46;     /* secondary accent */
  --bg:#fff8f0;        /* page background */
  --currency:'$';
  ...
}

Change --brand and --green and the whole site rebrands. Fonts: swap the Google Fonts <link> and the --font-head / --font-body names.

5. Reviews & socials

  • Replace the three review cards with real customer quotes (or delete the section until you have them).
  • In the <footer>, point Facebook/Instagram/TikTok links at your pages; delete the ones you don't use.

6. Put it online (free)

  • Netlify: app.netlify.com → drag the whole folder onto the page. Live in 30 seconds, free.
  • GitHub Pages / Vercel: also free — push the folder and enable Pages, or import on vercel.com.
